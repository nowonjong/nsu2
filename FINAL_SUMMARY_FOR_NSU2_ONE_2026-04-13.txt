  - glove fusion model uses the full vision+sensor input from the same capture.

7. Candidate model experiments
Candidate fusion variants were explored:
- sensor_correction_18w_v1
- sensor_correction_ambiguous_18w_v1
- modality_aware_correction_18w_v1
- modality_aware_rerank_18w_v1
Observed pattern:
- Some candidates look better on Practical-In / In-2.
- But they degrade on Practical-Out compared with baseline fusion.
- Therefore baseline fusion remains the safer final structure candidate for robustness.
